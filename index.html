About Me

I'm a passionate artist and indie game developer with a deep love for manga and anime aesthetics. My work focuses on creating heartfelt, expressive comics that tell meaningful stories.

I believe in the power of human creativity and craftsmanship, which is why all my work is 100% hand-drawn with no AI generation, though AI has its uses. I dislike wet socks, and like pie. Bent on delivering quality art in record time.

When I'm not drawing comics, I (try to) develope small games, and programming backend APIs.

Why choose this over AI

Simple and straightforward commission process

1

Legal Safety

AI tools may be trained on copyrighted art, and usage terms can be unclear. Hear you have clear ownership and use rights to what I draw.

2

More Privacy and Trust

AI collects prompt and image data, so what it makes isn't uniquely yours. Meanwhile I'm just one guy.

3

AI has troubles with comics

Atleast for now anyway. If a comic strip or page is done by an AI it won't be dynamic or consistent as opposed if it was done by a human, even if that human was a 9 year old.

4

Less Generic Art

While AI art can be good, it's trained from pre-existing data and styles This leads to art that has a very "AI did it" look. With human done art like mine it feels like something that is uniquely yours.
